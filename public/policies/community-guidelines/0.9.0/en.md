The SokDak community is a place where Korean-slang learners ask and answer questions together. These guidelines apply to every member who uses the community (posts, comments, photo attachments).

## 1. Prohibited conduct

The following are prohibited:

- Hate speech, discrimination, harassment, or threats against any person or group
- Content harmful to minors, such as sexually explicit material or graphic violence
- Spam, repetitive posting, advertising, promotion, or encouraging fraud or illegal activity
- Impersonating others or spreading false information
- Disrupting the normal operation of the service (e.g., mass posting with automated tools)

## 2. Reporting and blocking

- Every post and comment has a report button. Please report content you believe violates these guidelines.
- If you do not want to see a member's content, you can block that member. Blocked members' posts and comments no longer appear on your screen.
- Reports are reviewed by the operators, and the reporter's identity is not disclosed to the reported member.

## 3. Privacy

- Do not post personal information about yourself or others (real names, contact details, addresses, account information, etc.).
- Do not post photos or information that can identify another member without their consent.
- The service's handling of personal data is governed by the Privacy Policy.

## 4. Copyright

- Only post content you own or are licensed to use.
- Posting others' works (text, photos, videos, etc.) without permission may result in removal of the content, and further measures may be taken at the rights holder's request.

## 5. Operator actions

- Operators may hide or delete content that violates these guidelines.
- For repeated or serious violations, operators may restrict community access or suspend the account.
- Where a violation of law is suspected, operators may cooperate with the relevant authorities.

## 6. Appeals

- If you disagree with an operator action, you can appeal via My Page → Support in the app.
- Operators review each appeal and notify you of the outcome through a Support reply.

## 7. Changes to these guidelines

- When these guidelines change, a new version is published and takes effect on the stated effective date.
- To participate in the community after a new version takes effect, you must accept the updated guidelines again.

## 8. Contact

For questions about these guidelines, contact us via My Page → Support in the app.
